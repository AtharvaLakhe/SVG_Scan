Seven formats, one paste.

A tracer you can read in one sitting

No servers, no AI guesswork, no dependencies. About 1,000 lines of plain JavaScript turn pixels into paths in four passes, inside a Web Worker so the page never stutters.

  1. Quantize

    k-means++ in the OKLab color space picks a palette that matches what your eye sees, then every pixel snaps to its nearest color.

  2. Clean

    Anti-aliasing halos fold back into their neighbours, specks merge away, and slices of one gradient rejoin into a single shape.

  3. Trace

    Each color is walked along its pixel edges into closed loops, and tucks a few pixels under its neighbours so seams never show.

  4. Fit

    Real corners are snapped sharp, the rest is fit with least-squares Béziers, and shaded regions get linear or radial gradients.
